Financial Application Access Reconciliation

Every billing and ERP account is matched to the HR system of record and the role catalog, and each mismatch is classified by type, because the type decides who fixes it and how much audit exposure it creates.

Method

  1. Completeness first. Confirm each extract's record count against the application before testing; a partial extract makes every later result unreliable.
  2. Match to the system of record. Join accounts to HR by employee ID; anything that doesn't match is either a documented service account or an orphan.
  3. Compare life events with access. Termination and transfer dates are compared with account status and with each role's home department.
  4. Classify and assign. Each exception gets a type, a severity and an owner, then is traced by hand to the source record before it is reported.
