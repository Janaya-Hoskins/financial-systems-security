Incident Case File INC-77031: Self-Approved Billing Adjustments

A detection rule flagged off-hours billing credits approved by the same user who entered them. The investigation traced the activity to an emergency role grant with no expiry, contained it the next morning and fixed the control that allowed it.

SeverityHigh (financial impact, SoD bypass)
DetectedOct 19, 2025 07:30 by SIEM rule FIN-ADJ-07
ContainedOct 19, 2025 08:15 (45 minutes)
Impact6 credits, $48,300.00, 3 customer accounts

1 · Detection

FIN-ADJ-07 fires when an adjustment is self-approved, outside 07:00–19:00 or on a weekend, within 72 hours of the approver receiving the approval role. All three conditions were true for every adjustment in this case. The SQL and Splunk versions of the rule are in sql/financial_controls_tests.sql.

3 · Analysis

QuestionFindingEvidence
Was the activity authorized?No. The approval right came from an emergency request for month-end backlog, not for goodwill credits, and the user approved their own entries.Role change log EA-0412; adjustment log
How was SoD bypassed?The emergency grant added approval rights to an account that already had initiation rights. No SoD check runs on emergency grants, and the application doesn't block self-approval.Role assignments; workflow configuration
Was the session suspicious?Yes. VPN and application logins came from a host not in the asset inventory, after two failed application logins.Auth events; asset inventory
What was the pattern?6 credits, each under $10,000, alternating across 3 customer accounts in 25 minutes, all coded GOODWILL.Adjustment log

Root cause: emergency access was granted without an expiry or SoD check, and the billing application allowed initiator and approver to be the same account. Whether the activity was misuse of the employee's own access or of a compromised credential was referred to the investigations team; that determination is outside this case file.

4 · Containment and recovery

  1. 08:15 · approval role revoked and user sessions terminated (INC-77031).
  2. Credential reset and VPN access suspended pending investigation; unregistered host blocked at the VPN gateway.
  3. Finance placed holds on the 3 customer accounts and reversed the credits after review.
  4. Escalated to the Information Security lead, the Controller and Internal Audit with this case file attached.
