T1 · User access lifecycle (APD-02, APD-03)

Objective: access to billing and ERP is removed within 1 business day of termination and prior-department roles are removed within 5 business days of a transfer.
Risk: former employees or transferred staff keep the ability to create, adjust or post financial transactions.

Procedure

  1. Obtain the HR roster (system of record) and the account and role extracts for both applications as of Dec 31, 2025; confirm extract completeness against application record counts.
  2. Run scripts/reconcile_access.py to match every account to its HR owner and compare termination and transfer dates with account status and role grants.
  3. For each exception, confirm the HR event and the account state in the source records, and check for any activity after the event date.

T2 · Segregation of duties on billing adjustments (APD-07)

Objective: no person can both initiate and approve the same billing adjustment, under any account they hold.
Risk: unauthorized or erroneous credits post to customer balances without an independent check, misstating revenue and receivables.

Procedure

  1. Obtain the complete Q3–Q4 2025 adjustment log (2,410 records, $2,780,719.96) and reconcile its totals to the billing subledger.
  2. Run scripts/sod_check.py to flag records where initiator and approver are the same account, or two accounts owned by the same person.
  3. Inspect the application's workflow configuration to determine whether self-approval is blocked by the system.
