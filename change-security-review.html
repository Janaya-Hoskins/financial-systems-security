Security Impact Review: Billing Platform Upgrade

Security review of a major version upgrade of Northwind Holdings' billing platform, done with the application, infrastructure and PMO teams before the change advisory board decision. The method follows what an ERP or billing upgrade has to prove before go-live: access is unchanged, SoD still holds, the audit trail is continuous and the converted data reconciles.

ChangeCHG-2025-0931 (fictional)
SystemBilling (Oracle CC&B) major version upgrade
ClassificationSOX-relevant · high risk
RecommendationApprove with conditions

Scope of security review

  • Application roles and permissions after the upgrade, and the SoD rules that depend on them
  • Temporary privileged access for the cutover team
  • Audit logging, log forwarding to the SIEM and the FIN-ADJ-07 detection rule
  • Interface service accounts to the ERP general ledger
  • Data conversion of customer balances, unbilled usage and adjustment history
  • Approval workflow thresholds for billing adjustments

Conditions for approval

  1. Risks 1, 3, 5 and 6 have evidence on file before the change advisory board meets.
  2. Cutover privileged accounts (risk 2) have expiry dates set before they are created.
  3. Information Security reviews logging and the detection rule on the first business day after go-live.
  4. Internal Audit receives the evidence index, so SOX change-management testing can use it without a separate request.
